- **What worked:**
  - Probe-before-plan: the hero/Mountain/background/foreground/sky coverage from three renders showed that Mountain, not `background`, is the safe base, and that `Mountain − hero` also covers the shaded holes.
  - Hero-darker-than-surround (playbook §C): lift the hero a little (+0.08), calm the surround (−0.06), hero ends +2 L\* above the wall and keeps its chroma lead.
  - Cloning a hero **object mask with paint dabs** as a subtract component works (no wall burn on the spire at 1:1; fresh IDs on the Image and its nested Paint).
  - Radial ∩ Luminance Range − cloned hero, scripted: no mask edge or halo at 1:1; texture kept.
  - Moving the crop window down fixed the "no foreground / spire base cut" problem that a top trim could not.
- **What went wrong / lessons:**
  - My first plan included Dehaze +0.05 on the sky although my own calibration (engine §8) says it shows nothing below +0.25; the user asked what it would do.
  - Sky Highlights −0.08 gave −0.8 L\*, not my −3 forecast (9834 near-white ramp and 9751 whole-sky numbers do not transfer to smooth fog at L\* 81–88).
  - I described images to the user as "the picture above" in the terminal; the user saw nothing until I ran `open` (adapter says so).
  - The first crop (top kept) cut the spire's base; the hero mask reached the bottom row of the frame, which I only read after the user said the crop "still doesn't work".
  - The "strip on the right" was ambiguous after the crop moved; I annotated three candidate boxes and asked.
- **Noise:** not measured by me; the user zoomed to 100% after the final edit (shadow lifts, tree Exp +0.2625) and judged it not an issue — no denoise.
