- **Crop A** (light, ~5:4, non-destructive `delete:false`): doc px x 164–6717, y 246–5284 = 6553×5038. Bird on the left third, ~720 px of lead room vs ~105 behind; eye on the vertical centre line, not a power point (taste, kept).
- **Filter (4 corrections + user Remove):**
  - Subject (Select Subject, user): Exp +0.08, Shadows +0.10, Texture +0.20, Clarity +0.05 → deep darks +2.3, shadows +4.0.
  - Background (inverted subject): Exp −0.05, Texture −0.30, Clarity −0.10 → grass detail −28…−36%, grass −3.9 L*.
  - Eye (radial ∩ subject): Exp +0.05, Whites +0.05, Clarity +0.05, Texture +0.10 — **no measurable effect at 2000 px**; unverified.
  - Vignette (radial `MaskInverted=true`, feather 55, box larger than the frame, minus subject): Exp −0.06 → −3.2 L* at the corners, 0 at the centre.
  - Remove (user): one blurred grass stem above the head, painted along its full length (canvas (3500,720) → (2790,830) → (2200,2400)); 0.72% of pixels changed, bird box 23 px.
- **Net look:** grass 4.5–5.7 L* darker, a touch cooler/calmer (Δb* −0.6, C* −0.7), bird relatively brighter and crisper at the head. Mood is darker than the original — a taste call the user kept.
- **What worked:** subject mask cloned into inverted / subtract corrections (clean silhouette, bird protected to 0.00 ΔL*); Texture −0.30 as the background quieter; `CorrectionActive` bisect.
