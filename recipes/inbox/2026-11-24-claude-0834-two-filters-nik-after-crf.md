- **Agent:** claude
- **Target:** engine.md §5 (ACCESS: layer with two filters; `_index` order) + §2 (judge the final stack); WORKFLOW §2.7 (render after each filter, not only the Camera Raw one)
- **Type:** engine fact (ACCESS for the descriptor; DURABLE for "a global filter above a masked one undoes the masks")
- **Rule:** IF the document carries a second filter above the Camera Raw filter (here Nik 9 Color Efex, `com.dxo.Nik9.cep.ps`, unmasked) → DO (1) render the **whole stack**, (2) re-measure every region the masks calmed against the previous render (the strata band went 58.9 → 62.1 L\* when Darken/Lighten Center + Pro Contrast were added), (3) before any `ps_filter.js set`, list `filterFX[*].name` and confirm the target is the Camera Raw filter. → CHECK the Camera Raw `$LCs` is byte-equal to the last measured render before blaming it for a change.
