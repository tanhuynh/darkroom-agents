  - **An empty brush intersect killed the hero mask**: after the brush data was lost the hero lift read exactly 0 until the user deleted the 'Brush 1' component.
  - My first Shadows +0.15 lift gave +1.2 L\*; Exp +0.06 + Shadows +0.30 (Dehaze 0) gave +7 vs pass 2 (+5.5 over the original) — I had forecast +3.5 from pass 2 and forgot that dropping Dehaze removed a −5 darkening.

  - Narrowing the hero range removed the lift of fog above L\* 62 but not of fog at L\* 55 (same tone as rock).
  - `timeout` does not exist on macOS (use `perl -e 'alarm N; exec @ARGV'`).
