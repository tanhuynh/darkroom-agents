# 0025 — Grey crowned crane in dry grass, flat overcast (first wildlife case)

| genre | light | subject / elements | problems | location | filter JSON |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| wildlife | overcast, flat, no sky | grey crowned crane (left third, looking right), dry savanna grass | grass carried more visual weight than the bird; a blurred background stem; subject only mildly separated | East African savanna (not confirmed), edited 2026-11-17/18 | crf-0025-final.json |

- **Baseline (corrected):** median L* 46.4, grass hue 74–83° / C* 9.4 (warm tan), whites b* −3.2 at L* 74, darks (L* 10–20) b* −6.6 → mild shade-cool whites, warm grass = scatter, not a cast → **no raw-SO WB change** (AI Denoise only). Weight grid: Mid-Left 25%, Bot-Left 27%, Bot-Right 20% (foreground grass + bird body), bird neck 8%.
- **Crop A** (light, ~5:4, non-destructive `delete:false`): doc px x 164–6717, y 246–5284 = 6553×5038. Bird on the left third, ~720 px of lead room vs ~105 behind; eye on the vertical centre line, not a power point (taste, kept).
- **Order used:** Remove (user) → scripted passes 1–8 → Lens Blur (user) → final scripted write, all in one Camera Raw Filter on the raw SO.
- **Final filter (7 corrections + Remove + Lens Blur):**
  - **Subject** (Select Subject, user): Exp +0.08, Shadows +0.10, Texture +0.20, Clarity +0.05 → deep darks +2.3, shadows +4.0.
  - **Background** (inverted subject): Exp −0.05, Texture −0.30, Clarity −0.10, **Highlights −0.10, Saturation −0.05** (added in pass 4 after a Gemini benchmark of the same frame quieted the grass further) → grass detail −28…−36%, grass ~−5.5…−6.5 L* in total.
  - **Eye** (radial ∩ subject): Exp +0.05, Whites +0.05, Clarity +0.05, Texture +0.10 — read 0.00 at 2000 px but **works at 1:1**: eye box mean +1.5 L\*, brightest catchlight pixel 78.8 → 82.0, no glow.
  - **Vignette** (radial `MaskInverted=true`, feather 55, minus subject): Exp −0.06 → −3.2 L* at the corners.
  - **Pull** (horizontal left-edge gradient, minus subject): Exp −0.01 — **inactive in the final** (kept in the JSON for reference).
  - **Pull Copy** (the user's diagonal gradient toward the lower left, (0.23,0.34)→(0.11,0.80), minus subject): Exp −0.06, Highlights −0.04, Blacks −0.02 → bottom grass +2…+3 L* back from the user's −0.10; bottom-left grass ~31 L*. This is the active pull.
  - **Push** (large radial, L 0.22 R 1.02 T −0.45 B 0.55, feather 88, **no subject subtraction**): Exp +0.03, Highlights +0.05, Temperature +0.015, Dehaze −0.03 → head/beak +1.4 L*, upper neck +0.7, chest 0.00; upper-right grass +2.3 L*.
  - **Remove** (user): one blurred grass stem above the head, painted along its full length; 0.72% of pixels changed, bird box 23 px.
  - **Lens Blur** (user, BlurAmount 50, Highlights Threshold/Boost 50, hand-painted depth refinements): upper background detail −88%, tone unchanged (±0.05 L*); it does **not** soften the Push/Pull (four-way control: effect with blur on vs off ≤ 0.04 L*, map correlation 1.000). **Flaw in the saved file:** it blurs away fine feather tips and stray hairs sticking out past the subject (feather skirt, a stray hair on the neck) — left as is by the user (small at normal viewing size).
- **Net look:** darker, calmer, cooler grass (about 6 L* below the original at the bottom), blurred upper background, bird relatively brighter with a touch of warm light on the head. Mood is darker than the original — a taste call the user kept.
- **What worked:**
  - Subject mask cloned into inverted / subtract corrections (clean silhouette, bird protected to ±0.05 L*).
  - Texture −0.30 as the background quieter; Highlights/Saturation on the background added a step.
  - `CorrectionActive` bisect; the 1:1 halo check on the neck was clean.
  - **The user's diagonal pull**, aimed at where the weight sits (lower left), did far more than the horizontal edge pull — the lesson behind the §E rewrite.
  - A scripted write after the Lens Blur (every key resent, `$BigT` omitted) kept the depth strokes (tested on a saved copy).
- **What went wrong (process):**
  - The first baseline and "before" render were saved still in ProPhoto (Convert to Profile dialog cancelled, batch echoed SUCCESS), so the first critique called a healthy frame "dim, drab, cool-whited" and five bisect rounds chased a shift that did not exist.
  - The first background pass was too timid (no Highlights/Saturation).
  - The Push lit the grass more than the head, so on the lit side bird-vs-grass separation did not widen (subtracting the subject made it worse).
  - I first said the Push might read weaker after the Lens Blur because the texture was gone; the control showed no change at all, and the smooth background makes a +2 L* step more conspicuous, not less — hypothesis wrong.
  - I called the Eye correction "no measurable effect" from a 2000 px render; at 1:1 it is +1.5 L* with a brighter catchlight.
  - A Photoshop out-of-memory crash restored the document one pass behind (the Pull flag and Pull Copy value I had just set were lost); the document ID and folder path changed.
  - I once read an annotated preview grid at the wrong scale and measured the wrong box.
- **Checked on 2026-11-18 (four-way control on the final file, nothing saved):** Lens Blur vs Push/Pull (no interaction); Eye at 1:1 (works); Push with the subject subtracted (worse: head +0.3 instead of +1.3 L*, grass unchanged); bird edge at 1:1 (neck clean, feather tips eroded — see Lens Blur). **Decision:** the user left the eroded feather tips as they are ("good enough"); nothing open on this file. After a later re-save the Select Subject mask was recomputed again (new digests, same Origin); that did not restore the tips — the blur uses its own depth map, only the depth strokes change it. The JSON holds this latest state.
